— Our Story

A sanctuary for the timeless and the contemporary.

Founded in 1987 above a furniture workshop in the city's north quarter, Art Gallery Hub began as a single room with four paintings and a window that faced the canal. The founder, a retired conservator, wanted a space where a painting could be looked at for an hour without interruption.

Thirty-eight years later, the room has become six rooms. The canal is still there. The founding principle is the same: exhibit slowly, document honestly, and never confuse abundance with quality. Every work on these walls has been seen by at least three pairs of eyes before it was offered to a fourth.

Our vision is to remain small enough that every acquisition can be attributed to a personal decision, not a committee. Our mission is to publish a printed catalogue for each exhibition — because a painting, we believe, deserves more than a scroll.

— Featured Artists

The hands behind the canvas.

Four artists currently in stable representation. Each works slowly, exhibits rarely, and is collected by institutions we respect.

Elena Marchetti

Oil Painting

Milan-based painter working in the long tradition of Italian landscape. Her work has shown at the Venice Biennale (2019, 2022) and is held in three regional museums.

Tomas Reinhardt

Drawing & Charcoal

Berlin. Former architect; his drawings document the geometry of forgotten interiors. Solo shows at Hamburger Bahnhof and the Pinakothek der Moderne.

Yuki Tanaka

Mixed Media

Kyoto. Trained in the nihonga tradition, she incorporates gold leaf and mineral pigments into contemporary abstractions. Collected by the Mori Art Museum.

Amara Diop

Acrylic & Color Field

Dakar and Paris. Large-scale color field work examining diaspora memory. Collected by the Centre Pompidou and the Smith-Muyumba Foundation.
